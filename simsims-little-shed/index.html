You are Simsim. Waddle around your shed, share a coo with the flock, and watch the afternoon from a yellow perch.

Fly through the little trap to sit on the roof or explore Woodingdean freely. Use W/S forward/back, A/D sideways, and arrows up/down/left/right in flight. L lands on a nearby marked rooftop. R brings you home. Find a partner and build a family in your nesting box. Outside, explore the lawn, two stone stairways and patio; press L to land anywhere in the garden, or J/K to perch on the seated man's shoulder or lap when flying nearby.
